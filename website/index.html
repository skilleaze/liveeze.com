Our story

A beautiful space should never be hard to live in, or hard to create.

The name says it. LIVE is how people live and work in a space. EZE is ease: an effortless space and an effortless process. The double E stands for Elegance and Ease.

Our monogram tells the same story: the upright is architecture and permanence, the swash is design and comfort, and the taper runs from structure to the finest detail.

You keep the joy of creating a space. We carry the complexity.
