Our story

A beautiful space should never be hard to live in, or hard to create.

LIVEEZE was founded on a simple observation. Most clients who set out to build a home or a workplace end up managing a crowd: an architect, a designer, a contractor, a lighting specialist, a dozen suppliers. The vision gets diluted between them.

We brought every discipline under one roof, so a single team carries each idea from the first sketch to the final key.

You keep the joy of creating a space. We carry the complexity.

LIVE

To live, to dwell. Every project begins with how people will live and work in a space, not with how it will look in a photograph.

EZE

Ease in two senses: a finished space that feels effortless, and a process that takes the weight off the client.

The double E

Where the two words meet stand our two standards: Elegance and Ease.

LIVE EXQUISITELY.

Exquisite, from the Latin exquisitus: carefully sought out. Every material selected, every light placed, every detail considered on your behalf.

Questions

Good to know.

What does LIVEEZE do?

We are a full-service design and build studio. One team covers interior architecture, 3D visualisation, furniture, lighting, materials, landscape, contracting and turnkey delivery for residential and commercial spaces.

Can I hire you for design only, or for a single service?

Yes. You can choose one service, such as design, 3D visualisation, furniture or consulting, or let us take the whole project from concept to handover.

Do you work on homes and on commercial spaces?

Both. We design and build villas, homes and apartments, and commercial spaces including offices, restaurants, cafés, lobbies and building exteriors. You can browse each area in our portfolio.

Will I see my space before it is built?

Yes. Our exterior and interior 3D visualisation shows your space photorealistically, so you can make decisions on layout, materials and lighting with confidence before work begins.

How much does a project cost, and how long does it take?

Every space is different. After the first consultation we prepare a proposal with the scope, budget and timeline for your project.

Do you design outdoor spaces?

Yes. Landscape planning covers gardens, balconies and terraces, with planting, paving, seating and outdoor lighting.

How do we start?

Send us an enquiry using the form below. We will reply to arrange a first consultation, where we listen to your needs before we draw anything.
