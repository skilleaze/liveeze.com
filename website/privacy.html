Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to information when you use liveeze.com.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not create accounts.

Enquiries

The enquiry form does not send or store anything on this website. When you press “Send enquiry”, your own email app opens with a message addressed to info@liveeze.com. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. We use the details you send only to reply to your enquiry and to discuss your project.

Sound preference

If you mute or unmute the site’s sound, that choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it on your next visit. It is not sent to us.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted by Vercel, and its typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any web server, these services receive basic technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages.

Contact

For any question about your information, write to info@liveeze.com.
